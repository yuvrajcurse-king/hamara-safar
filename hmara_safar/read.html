ARC 1: EK ANJAANI SHURUAAT

Preface

Ek Anjaani Shuruaat

Kabhi kabhi zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat kahaniyan ek chhoti si mulaqaat se shuru hoti hain. Ye kahani bhi ek aise hi safar ki dastaan hai.

Acknowledgements

Main un sab doston, teachers aur readers ka dhanyavaad karta hoon jinhone meri writing ko padhkar mujhe aur likhne ki himmat di.

About the Author

Jay ek student aur poet writer hai. Use kahaniyan, shayari aur emotions ko shabdon mein pirohna pasand hai. "Humara Safar: Ek Adhoori Dastaan" uski pehli novel hai.

Pehli Nazar

Chapter 1

wo din mujhe aaj bhe yaad ha,
Jab maine use Pehli baar dekha tha

District level ka match chal raha tha. Hall mein sab players aur coach the, aur wahan par mujhe nahi pata tha ki meri zindagi badalne wali thi.

Udhar match ho raha tha. Main apni team ke players ko support kar raha tha. Sab kuch aam tha. Lekin phir meri nazar us par padi. Us waqt mujhe nahi pata tha ki woh kaun hai aur mujhe kyu kuch ajeeb lag raha hai.

Phir achanak mera dost mujhe bola, "Jay, kahan khoya hai? Udhar tera match hai, aur players bhi aa chuke hain."

Jaise hi main samjha, main unke paas chala gaya. Phir maine apna match khela aur silver aaya tha.

Phir last mein mujhe pata chala ki woh to hamari team mein thi, aur hamare school mein bhi hai.

Mujhe pata nahi kyu, lekin us waqt main bahut khush tha aur us din mein kuch alag tha.

Lekin zindagi aksar wahi kahaniyan likhti hai,
jinhe hum pehli nazar mein samajh nahi paate...

National Level

Chapter 2

Yeh National ka time tha, ya kahun use se pehli baar baat karne ka time tha.

Mujhe yaad hai, maine use white T-shirt, khule hue baal aur sundar si aankhon mein dekha. Mujhe aisa laga jaise waqt ruk sa gaya ho.

Jay: "Hey, suno!" ~: "Haan bolo, Jay." Jay: "Tum mujhe jaanti ho?" ~: "Haan, tum hamari team mein ho, isliye jaanti hoon." ~: "Haan, main tumhara naam jaan sakta hoon?" ~: "Haan, kyu nahi?" Soniya: "Ayy!" ~: "Haan bolo, Soniya." Soniya: "Udhar chalo jaldi, ek kaam hai." ~: "Chalo." ~: "Jay, main tumse baad mein milti hoon."

Phir usne Jay ki taraf dekha aur chali gayi.

Phir udhar match ho raha tha aur main to use hi dekhta ja raha tha. Jaise meri nazar us se hat hi nahi rahi ho.

Main apna semi-final match khel raha tha. Sab mujhe support kar rahe the. Soniya bhi kar rahi thi. Woh meri behen jaisi thi. Main jeet bhi gaya aur woh bhi dekh rahi thi.

Phir National ke baad hum ghar aa rahe the. Use waqt to kuch khaas hua nahi, lekin main to use ke khayal mein tha.

Mera dost bol raha ha ke, "Tujhe kya hua hai? Tu kahin khoya hua lagta hai aaj kal."

"Main hans kar baat taal gaya, kyunki main kis ko batata ki main kis ke baare mein soch raha tha."

Aur use to us waqt tak kuch pata bhi nahi tha ki...

Wo Mere School Mein Thi

Chapter 3

Mujhe jab pata chala ki wo mere school mein ha, to main bahut khush hua.

Mai Class 5th mein tha. Main player tha aur thoda famous bhi tha. Phir 5th period mein sir achanak se bole:

Sir: "Abe oye Jay, kya kar raha hai? Yahan Maths ka hard question hai aur tum..." Jay: "Sir, sorry... main thoda..." Sir: "chro, Iska answer batao." Jay: "sir, Answer..." Piyu: "Bhi, answer √2 hai." Jay: "Sir, √2 answer hai." Sir: "Jay, sach mein?" Jay: "Piyu, sahi hai na?" Piyu: "Haan bhi." Jay: "Haan sir." Sir: "Good. Ab samajh aaya? Chalo, 20 push-ups aur 30 second ka hold karo." Jay: "Sir, kya answer galat tha?" Sir: "Haan, Jay. Chalo, aage." Puri class hansne lagi Jay: "Piyu..." Piyu: "Bhi, galti se 2 ka answer bata diya."

Phir maine 20 push-ups aur 30 second ka hold kiya.

Break mein mere do dost bole:

Dost: "Jay, kahin khoya hua hai kya aaj kal?" Wo mere class friends the. Jay: "Bhi, mujhe lagta hai main ek ladki ko pasand karta hoon." Dost: "Kaun hai? Bata na." Jay: "Naam to pata nahi, lekin wo hamare school mein hai. wo hamare team ma ha ."

Ek dost bola: "To jaakar list dekh le."

Jay: "hmm"

Break khatam hone se pehle Jay corridor ma bhaaga. Or notice board ki taraf, Wahan notice board par students ki list lagi thi. Jay ne jaldi-jaldi list dekhni shuru ki.

Phir uski nazar ek naam par jaakar ruk gayi...

"Shree - Class 7th"

Ek pal ke liye Jay chup ho gaya. Uska liya waqt ruke gaya ho or shree uska samna ho. Shree ko dhakta he, jay ma akho ma asu aa gaya.

Tabhi Piyu wahan aa gaya.

Piyu: "Bhi, naam pata chala?" Jay: "Haan... Shree. Class 7th." Piyu: "Bhi, wo to senior hai tujhse." Jay: "Haan... pata hai."

Thodi der ke liye dono chup rahe. Lekin Jay ke chehre par ek alag si khushi thi.

Thodi der baad Jay ne door se Shree ko dekha. Wo apni class ki taraf ja rahi thi.

Jay chup tha, lekin uski nazar baar-baar shree ke taraf chali jaati thi.

Ab Jay use door ya kahi se base shree ko dekhta tha. Break mein, corridor mein, ya school ke kisi bhi kone mein.

Phir Class 5th yahin par khatam hui. Final exams bhi ho gaye. Ab Jay 6th mein tha aur Shree 8th mein.

Lekin kahani abhi shuru hui thi.

Us waqt Jay ko nahi pata tha ki ye sirf ek chhoti si pasand hai ya uski zindagi ki sabse badi kahani banne wali hai.

Aur...
Jay ko Shree se pyaar ho gaya tha ya.. hone laga tha.
