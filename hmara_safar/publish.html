PUBLISH YOUR WORK

Apni kahaani yahan likho

Koi bhi student, writer ya artist yahan apna story, poem, shayari, comic idea ya short piece bhej sakta hai. Approved works Hamara Safar community wall par dikhaye jaayenge.

Guidelines

Simple rules so everyone’s work stays respectful and original.

  • Original work only — apna likha / banaya hua content bhejo.
  • Language: Hindi, English, or Hinglish — sab welcome.
  • No hate, bullying, or adult content. School-friendly vibes.
  • Max ~3000 words for stories; poems/shayari can be shorter.
  • Optional: Instagram / Google Drive link if you have art or long text.
  • Review usually takes a few days. You’ll get a note if contact email is given.
Submit your piece

Form Netlify par save hota hai. Free tier: ~100 submissions / month.

How it works
  • 1. Submit — form is saved securely on Netlify.
  • 2. Review — team (Yuvraj / Jay) checks originality & guidelines.
  • 3. Publish — approved pieces appear on the Community Wall (coming soon on this page) or Instagram.
  • 4. Credit — your name stays with the work. You keep full rights.
